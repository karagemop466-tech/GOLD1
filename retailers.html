Named but not vetted

Costco, James Allen, Tiffany & Co., Aurate and Derco Diamonds were encountered while sourcing and are deliberately not scored here. No price from any of them appears in this dataset. Absence is not a negative finding — it is an open task (flag F-15).

Contact and returns are the two fastest filters

A seller who will not give a phone number, a written weight and a return window in writing fails the checklist before price is even discussed. All three retailers here do publish a contact route; only Mejuri publishes a confirmed 30-day window on the pages read.

No invented numbers. Every price here was read from the retailer's own page on 2026-10-22 and is timestamped. Blank cells are blank because the seller does not publish the value — not because it was estimated.
