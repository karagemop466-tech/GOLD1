Melt calculator (arithmetic over the inputs you see)

gold value (USD) = weight (g) × purity × spot (USD/ozt) ÷ 31.1034768. The divisor is the NIST-published troy ounce gram figure ("31.103 476 8", quoted on Sources). Purity uses the exact karat fraction (karat ÷ 24); NIST's chart notes a 24K mark means 99.9% pure. This ignores alloy/side stones, labor, retail markup — it is the raw gold number only.

How the checklist uses this

Price ÷ raw-gold-value tells you how much of the price is metal vs craft/stones — an absurd ratio (price ≪ melt, or price many× melt without disclosure) is a walk-away signal. Compare on Checklist item 6.
