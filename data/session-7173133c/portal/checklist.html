The 12-point solid-gold buying checklist

1. Gold purity: confirm the karat — 10K, 14K, 18K, 22K, or 24K. Higher karat = more fine gold, softer metal.

verified All 71 rows across 11 retailers state an explicit legal karat or 750/1000 hallmark fineness on the seller's official page, covering every legal tier: 4 in 10K (41.7% gold), 50 in 14K (58.3%), 11 in 18K (75.0%), 4 in 22K (91.7%, via Auvere), and 2 in 24K pure gold (99.9%+, via Menē). Under 16 CFR § 23.3(a)–(b), unqualified "Gold" means 24K only; alloys below 24K must disclose karat.

How verified: Karat read directly from each official product page/feed and verified against the row's verbatim evidence quote by automated checks S03, A02, and H01.

Your action: Choose 14K or 18K for prong-set engagement rings (22K and 24K are high-purity softer alloys best suited to bands or protective bezels; 10K is hardest with least gold), and verify stamp + assay on arrival.

Sources: eCFR 16 CFR Part 23 (§ 23.3) · All verified rings

2. Solid gold: the listing must explicitly say solid gold — not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay, gold-tone, or hollow.

open 21 of 71 listings explicitly state "solid gold" in the seller's own text (8 Automic Gold, 7 Mejuri, 4 Auvere 22K, Ross-Simons 997811, and Aurate Diamond X Ring); the remaining 50 state karat only. Critically, under FTC 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) & fn. 32, an item with a hollow center may NOT legally be called "Solid Gold" — yet Mejuri's FAQ and Bold Chain Ring ($348) call hollowed gold "solid gold" (flagged). Ross-Simons search results also mix solid gold with "18kt/22kt Gold Over Sterling" plated silver (excluded, Flag F57).

How verified: Checked all 71 product pages for "solid", hollow construction disclosures, and plating/vermeil/gold-filled/overlay terms (automated checks S05 & S07), cross-referenced against 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) and § 23.4.

Your action: Get one written sentence before paying: "Solid [10K/14K/18K/22K] gold throughout — non-hollow, non-electroformed, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled."

Sources: Flagged F01 & hollow-gold conflict · 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) & § 23.4

3. Hallmark: check for a 10K/14K/18K/22K/24K (or 417/585/750/916/999) purity stamp PLUS a registered maker's trademark.

not-online Zero online product pages display a macro photograph or verbatim transcript of the interior shank hallmark + maker's trademark (Cartier states "750/1000" fineness in text; Menē states 24K hallmarking on its COA). Under the National Gold and Silver Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 297(b)) and 16 CFR § 23.10, any quality mark MUST be closely accompanied by a U.S. registered/applied-for trademark or legal manufacturer name (with 3/1000 unsoldered and 7/1000 soldered gold assay tolerances under 15 U.S.C. § 295).

How verified: Inspected all 71 product pages and verified 16 CFR § 23.10 + 15 U.S.C. §§ 295, 297 verbatim on 2026-10-23.

Your action: Request an interior-band photo before purchase, and inspect under a 10× loupe on day one for BOTH the karat/fineness mark AND the maker's registered trademark.

Sources: 15 U.S.C. § 297 (Trademark rule) · 15 U.S.C. § 295 (Assay tolerances) · Flagged F20

4. Weight: check the ring's weight in grams — a legitimate seller should state or quote it unambiguously.

partial 25 of 71 rows (25 active + 1 withdrawn) publish an unambiguous metal gram weight on the official product page (0.3 g to 7.0 g across Ross-Simons, Automic Gold, and Aurate in 10K, 14K, and 18K). The remaining 46 rows are left blank rather than guessed — including Auvere's 4 22K rings whose PDPs label size-dependent ranges "Approximate Fine Weight" (ambiguous between 22K alloy mass and 24K fine content, Flag F54) and Menē's 2 JS-rendered 24K bands.

How verified: Opened every HTML product page across all 71 listings; enforced by automated checks S14, A03, H03, and H04.

Your action: For any of the 46 unweighted rows (including Auvere's "Approximate Fine Weight" listings), ask customer service in writing for the exact gross alloy gram weight for your ring size before paying.

Sources: Weight & melt analysis · Flagged F02 & F54

7. Return policy: confirm full-refund window and exclusions on resized, engraved, tag-removed, or custom rings.

verified Official return policies verified across all 11 retailers: Menē (7d receipt, up to 10% restocking fee), Anna Sheffield (14d delivery, 10% restocking up to $500), Auvere (15d delivery, 10d dropoff, security tag must stay untouched — contradicting older 30d Journal post, Flag F55), Aurate (policy says 14d fulfillment, but ring PDPs state "Final Sale! Exchange for size only"), Blue Nile (30d from SHIPMENT, max 2/yr), Brilliant Earth (30d ship-readiness, $40 engraving fee), With Clarity (30d receipt, max 1/6 mos), Ross-Simons (30d receipt), Mejuri (30d delivery), Automic Gold (30d delivery), and Cartier (30d delivery). Every seller voids returns if resized or altered by a third-party jeweller.

How verified: Read all 11 official refund/terms pages directly through 2026-10-23; stored in data/retailers.json.

Your action: Never engrave at checkout, never remove security tags, never let an outside jeweller resize or scratch-test during the return window, and note shipment-based clocks (Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth) and short 7–15 day clocks (Menē, Anna Sheffield, Auvere).

Sources: Auvere shipping & returns (15d) · Blue Nile returns (shipment clock) · Menē terms (7d window)

8. Payment: use a credit card for statutory dispute protection; avoid wire, debit, crypto, or cash-equivalent BNPL.

verified Verified against official CFPB Fair Credit Billing Act guidance (15 U.S.C. § 1666 & § 1666i; Ask-CFPB en-61 & en-1969): credit cards grant a 60-day written billing-error dispute right and statutory "claims and defenses" rights on >$50 purchases after a good-faith merchant attempt (statutorily tied to home state or ≤100 miles, though many issuers extend broader network chargeback protection). Debit cards, wires, Zelle, and non-credit BNPL do not carry FCBA rights.

How verified: Retrieved CFPB Ask-CFPB articles en-61 and en-1969.

Your action: Pay directly with a major credit card, save screenshots of checkout terms, and send written billing disputes within 60 days if misrepresented goods are refused for return.

Sources: CFPB billing-error disputes (en-61) · CFPB unsatisfactory goods (en-1969)

10. Upon arrival: before wearing, engraving, removing security tags, or resizing, inspect at an independent jeweller inside the return window.

buyer-action Physical inspection cannot be done online — and return windows are as short as 7 days (Menē), 14 days (Anna Sheffield), 15 days with intact security tag (Auvere), or 30 days from shipment rather than delivery (Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth). Any third-party resize, prong work, tag removal, or acid file scratch test voids returns.

How verified: Cross-checked physical inspection steps against all 11 retailers' return-voiding clauses.

Your action: Book an independent gemologist/appraiser for day one after delivery; insist strictly on non-destructive testing (calibrated 0.01 g scale, 10× loupe hallmark read, hydrostatic specific gravity, ultrasound, and XRF) with security tags and certificates completely untouched.

Sources: Flagged F12 & F55

11. Professional purity testing: pair XRF with scale weight & specific-gravity density — never rely on stamp or surface XRF alone.

verified GIA Gems & Gemology Lab Notes prove neither a hallmark NOR standalone surface XRF is sufficient on its own: in Spring 2024 (Vol. 60, No. 1), a 41 g resin-filled electroformed bracelet with a glued-on "14K" disk read 17.99K–22.47K on XRF with no plating alert; in Summer 2025 (Vol. 61, No. 2, "Gold-Plated Gold"), 9K/10K gold rings plated with higher-karat yellow gold read 13.5K–18K on XRF because X-rays penetrate only microns. Combining XRF with precision gram weight + hydrostatic specific-gravity (density) testing catches hollow/resin cores and under-karated interiors non-destructively.

How verified: Retrieved and verified GIA Spring 2024 and Summer 2025 Lab Notes directly from gia.edu.

Your action: Ask the independent appraiser for BOTH XRF fluorescence AND hydrostatic specific-gravity + calibrated gram weight, and keep the written test report.

Sources: GIA 2024 — Electroformed Gold Bracelet · GIA 2025 — Gold-Plated Gold

The key

A stamp is a claim, not proof. Reputable seller + correct markings (karat + maker's mark) + price consistent with weight × karat × spot + independent testing after delivery — and walk away if verification or a return is refused.
