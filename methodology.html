Method

Short version: read the seller's own machine-readable catalogue, copy the numbers, link the page, and let a script hunt for anything that does not add up.

No hallucinations — the rule this whole project runs on

Every price, karat and weight below is copied from the seller's own product feed and linked on the same row. Nothing is estimated, averaged or remembered. If a value was not confirmed on an official source it is shown as — unverified and listed on the Flagged for review page. Re-check against the source link before you buy: prices change without notice.

1. Where the data comes from

The four retailers in scope publish machine-readable product feeds at /products.json and /search/suggest.json. Those endpoints are the seller's own catalogue — the same data their product page renders — which makes them the strongest available primary source short of a signed quote. Prices, titles, metal descriptions and variant ranges were read from those feeds on 2026-10-22. Policy text was read from the retailers' own /policies/refund-policy pages. The gold price was read from a market-data publisher and cross-checked against COMEX futures.

Nothing was read from a search-engine snippet, a shopping comparison, an influencer round-up or a review site. Those are secondary at best and are exactly where invented prices come from.

2. What was excluded on purpose

  • Set & Stones — its engagement-ring results were gold-filled, which fails the solid-gold rule. Excluded rather than ranked.
  • Blue Nile — the category URL tested returned a 404, so no row could be sourced and none was invented.
  • Vermeil variants — Aurate sells vermeil and solid gold on the same product page. The vermeil prices are not used anywhere in the table.
  • Settings priced as rings — products tagged "Ring Setting Product" are labelled as setting-only, never presented as a complete ring price.

3. The verification passes

After every review pass the suite in scripts/verify.mjs runs and its output is published on Checks. It is not a vibe check; each test is mechanical:

  1. Provenance. Every row must have an HTTPS source URL on the retailer's own domain, a retrieval date, and an evidence string.
  2. Price appears in its own evidence. The price digits must occur inside the evidence text captured from the feed. A price that is not in the evidence is treated as fabricated.
  3. Karat validity. Karat must be one of 10/14/18/22/24 and must also appear in the row's evidence.
  4. Arithmetic. Purity must equal karat ÷ 24; price_low must not exceed price_high; melt value must never exceed the asking price for a published weight.
  5. Solid-claim honesty. A row may only claim "solid stated" if the evidence text actually contains the word "solid".
  6. No future dates. Retrieval dates later than the build date fail the run.
  7. Links. Every internal link and asset reference in the generated HTML must resolve to a file that exists.
  8. No silent blanks. Any null value must be accompanied by a flag explaining it.

4. Limits you should know

  • Prices move. The numbers are a snapshot from 2026-10-22. The retailer page always wins.
  • Weight is mostly missing. 1 of 30 listings publish grams. The value comparison cannot be completed for the rest, and this site refuses to fill that gap with an estimate.
  • Hallmarks are physical. No stamp can be verified from a web page. A photo of the stamp is evidence of what is written, not proof of what the metal is. XRF or an assay by an independent jeweller is the only real test.
  • Reputation is not scored here. Seller verification in this pass means: official store identity plus a verifiable phone or support email. It is not a rating and it is not an endorsement.
  • Four US retailers. This is a curated sample, not the whole market. Coverage is stated so you can judge the gap.

5. Reproducing the run

The collector in scripts/collect.mjs re-fetches every configured source and writes a timestamped snapshot to data/raw/. The workflow in .github/workflows/collect.yml runs it on demand. The curated rows in data/rings.json are checked against those snapshots by scripts/verify.mjs, and scripts/build.mjs regenerates every page. No paid services, no CDN, no tracking scripts.

No hallucinated prices. Every price and specification on this site was copied from an official retailer feed retrieved on 2026-10-22 and is linked to the exact page it came from. Values that could not be confirmed are blank, not estimated.
