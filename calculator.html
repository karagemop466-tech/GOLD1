Gold value calculator

Two numbers decide whether a gold ring is fairly priced: how many grams it weighs, and what gold is worth that day. This calculator does the arithmetic. It does not guess any weight — you have to get that from the seller or a scale.

Formula: grams × (karat ÷ 24) × (price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768). Stones and labour are excluded, so real jewellery normally costs 2–6× its melt value. A multiple under 1 means the price is below the gold content — treat that as a red flag, not a bargain.

Worked example from this list

The Aurate Modern Cigar Band is the only listing here that publishes a weight: 2.6–4 g at 14K, listed at $1,308.00. That is $196.42–$302.18 of gold, or about 4.3× the top of the metal range. For a wide designer band that is a normal retail multiple, and it is checkable — which is the point.
