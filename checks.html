Verification checks

An automated pass runs after every review. It is built to catch the specific ways a price or specification gets invented: a number with no source, a karat that contradicts its evidence, a melt value higher than the asking price, or a link that goes nowhere.

No hallucinations — the rule this whole project runs on

Every price, karat and weight below is copied from the seller's own official product page or feed and linked on the same row. Nothing is estimated, averaged, inferred or remembered. If a value was not confirmed on an official source it is shown as — unverified and listed on the Flagged for review page. Every build is mechanically verified by automated anti-hallucination and security passes (S01–S20 + A01–A10 + H01–H13 + N01–N11 + Q01–Q10), and every row is re-read live against its official page with the result published on the evening live audit. We actively recommend that you look for hallucinations yourself: open any row's official product link and check our verbatim evidence quote against the live seller page before buying (latest re-read: 2026-10-23; snapshots are not live quotes).
