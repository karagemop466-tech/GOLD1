Methodology & Sources

Verification rules (applied to every value on this site)

  1. Official source or nothing. Every price/karat/weight must come from the official retailer page or an official institution (eCFR, govinfo, gold.org, assay offices). Third-party blogs are never used for values.
  2. Direct fetch beats snippet. A value read by downloading the official page is 'Verified'. A value seen only in a search-engine snippet of that page is recorded as a note but rendered BLANK until directly confirmed.
  3. Evidence quotes. Every verified value stores the exact quote from the page, its URL and fetch date (data/*.json in the repo).
  4. Blank means blank. Unverifiable values (e.g. metal weight in grams) are shown as “—” and flagged — never estimated or inferred.
  5. Re-run after every review. python3 scripts/verify_data.py re-checks schema, evidence, arithmetic and links, and writes data/audit_report.json. Find a hallucination → fix data → re-run.
  6. Prices are dated snapshots. Retail and gold prices move; every number carries its “checked” date.
