Gold calculator

What is the gold actually worth?

Arithmetic only: weight × purity × spot. It cannot tell you whether a ring is a good buy, and it refuses to run on guessed numbers — you need a real gram weight from the seller.

Inputs

From the seller, in writing, or from a jeweler's scale. Not published on any listing in this dataset.

Karat is a 24-part scale: fineness = karat ÷ 24.

Default is the recorded reading: $4,026.79 on Oct 22nd, 2026 at 07:07 NY Time from GoldPrice.org. Edit it to today's number — the result is stamped with whatever you enter.

Compare the asking price with the melt value to see the multiple.

Result

Fine gold content — enter a weight to begin
Melt value at your spot price — not a valuation of the ring
Gold value as a share of the price — add a price to compare

Formula

fine gold (g) = weight (g) × (karat ÷ 24)

melt value = fine gold (g) ÷ 31.1034768 × spot (USD/oz)

share = melt value ÷ price asked

1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams exactly. Karat fractions (10/24, 14/24, 18/24) are arithmetic, not an assay.

Why the result is not a price opinion

A ring's price includes fabrication, the setting, the stone, labour, duty, warranty and the retailer's margin. Melt value is what the gold alone would fetch as scrap. On a diamond ring the two numbers can sit far apart, and neither one is “the real price”.

Melt value is also not what a jeweler will pay: refining costs and trade margins apply.
