Gold Value Calculator

Gold metal-value calculator

Weight × purity × spot = approximate melt (metal) value of the gold in a ring. It excludes brand, design, labour, gemstones and retail margin — real rings cost far more than melt; fake "gold" rings often cost suspiciously less.

Spot prefilled from the project anchor: $4,024.36/ozt, World Gold Council data as of 2026-10-21 (gold.org ↗) — update it to today's value before deciding anything.

Conversions used: 1 troy oz = 31.1034768 g; 1 dwt = 1/20 ozt = 1.55517384 g. Purity = karat ÷ 24.

Per-gram value by karat at the prefilled spot

A fair scrap offer is typically below these numbers (refiner margin). A retail price is typically far above them. Below-melt pricing on a "solid gold" ring = classic red flag (checklist C6).

Limits of this estimate

  • Estimates metal value only — it is not an appraisal.
  • Actual gold content requires testing (XRF / fire assay — checklist C11); a stamp is only a claim.
  • Gold-bearing alloys also contain prongs/solder of lower fineness; US tolerance is ≤7/1000 below the mark for rings (15 U.S.C. §295 — see prices page).
