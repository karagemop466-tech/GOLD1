Online Gold-Ring Buying Checklist

The key

A 14K or 18K stamp by itself does NOT prove purity. The safe pattern: reputable seller + correct markings (karat + maker's mark) + price consistent with weight × karat × spot + independent testing after delivery.

Anchors: 16 CFR Part 23 (FTC Jewelry Guides) ↗ · 15 U.S.C. ch. 8 — National Gold and Silver Stamping Act ↗ · Gold spot anchor — World Gold Council ↗

The 12-point checklist

#RuleDetail / verified findingsAnchored to
C1Gold purity — confirm the karat (10K, 14K, 18K...). Higher karat = more gold.Karat = parts of gold per 24. 18K = 75.0%, 14K = 58.3%, 10K = 41.7%. See the Gold Prices & Karats page for the full table.16 CFR §23.3 — karat fineness must be correctly disclosed ↗
C2Solid gold — listing must explicitly say SOLID gold — not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, gold overlay, or gold-tone.Vermeil = gold-plated silver. Gold-filled/rolled gold plate = mechanically bonded surface layer (≥1/40th of article weight at ≥10K for the mechanical plating example in the Guides). If a listing can't say 'solid 14K gold' or 'solid 18K gold', walk away.16 CFR §23.3(b)(3),(5)-(8), (c)(1)-(2) — plated/filled/overlay terms are surface coatings with their own fineness rules; hollow pieces may not be called 'solid' ↗
C3Hallmark — look for a 10K/14K/18K stamp PLUS a maker's/manufacturer's mark.'...who applies or causes to be applied a quality mark... shall apply to the article the trademark or name of such person.' A karat stamp with NO maker's mark is a red flag. UK-sold rings carry independent assay-office hallmarks (e.g. London, hallmarking since 1300).eCFR §23.10 quoting the National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. §297) ↗
C4Weight — check the ring's weight in grams. A legitimate seller should be able to provide it.FINDING: none of the 14 listings reviewed publishes metal weight in grams (see Flag F1). Request it in writing; use the calculator page to convert weight + karat into metal value.Project checklist; supported by 16 CFR §23.18 (weight misrepresentation is deceptive) ↗
C5Seller reputation — research independent reviews and complaints; verify real contact information.Also verify corporate changes: e.g. James Allen now routes to Blue Nile (Flag F8) — know exactly WHICH legal entity you are buying from. US sellers operating deceptively on metal content face FTC Act §5 enforcement via the Jewelry Guides.Project checklist
C6Price — compare the price with the ring's gold weight and current gold value. Unusually cheap = red flag.Use the calculator: metal melt value = grams × (karat/24) × spot-per-gram. Retail settings are priced far above melt (design, brand, labour, stones) — but BELOW-melt pricing on a 'gold' ring is a classic fake signal.Project checklist; anchor price on Gold Prices page (World Gold Council, fetched 2026-10-22) ↗
C7Return policy — make sure you can return it after receiving and inspecting it; check exclusions on resized/custom rings.Verified examples 2026-10-22: Mejuri free 30-day returns & exchanges; Blue Nile free returns; Cartier complimentary returns & exchanges; Brilliant Earth free shipping & returns + free 1-year resizing; Tiffany — CONFLICTING text flagged (F2): confirm in writing before buying. Engraving/resizing often final-sale (Mejuri engraving: not eligible for returns).Project checklist; compare with retailer policies found
C8Payment — prefer a credit card for purchase protection; avoid irreversible payment methods.Irreversible methods (wire, crypto, gift cards) leave no chargeback path. Note: financing plans seen on listings (Affirm/Klarna/Blue Nile card) are credit products, not purchase protection.Project checklist
C9Documentation — keep the listing, receipt, description, and the seller's claims about gold purity.Screenshot the listing including any 'solid 14K/18K gold' wording — the FTC Guides make metal-content claims part of the product's lawful description, so your record is your evidence.Project checklist
C10Upon arrival — before wearing or resizing, take it to an independent jeweler to verify gold purity and weight.Do this INSIDE the return window. Resizing before verification can forfeit your return rights (see C7 exclusions).Project checklist
C11Best verification — ask for XRF testing or another professional purity test; don't rely on the stamp.XRF (X-ray fluorescence) reads surface composition non-destructively; fire assay is destructive but definitive. A stamp is only a claim — 15 U.S.C. §294 makes FALSE marks unlawful, but enforcement happens after the fact; test the piece.Project checklist; independent assay is the job of assay offices (e.g. London Assay Office) ↗
C12If the seller won't let you independently verify/return it — don't buy.This is the project's bright line. A confident seller of real solid gold has nothing to fear from a jeweler's scale and an XRF read.Project checklist
