No hallucinations

The rule this whole project runs on

  1. No invented prices. Every figure was read from the retailer's own product or listing page on 2026-10-22 and carries that date. Search snippets are treated as untrusted — two of them were wrong (flags F-02, F-03).
  2. No inferred values. If a seller does not publish a weight or a hallmark, the cell is empty and flagged. We never fill a gap from a similar ring, a blog, or memory.
  3. No silent estimates. The gold calculator is arithmetic on numbers you supply. It is not a valuation and no row in this dataset carries a computed melt value.
  4. Every claim is re-checkable. 13 rows, 13 registered sources, and an automated audit that recomputes every headline statistic before the site builds.

What the data actually shows

Three findings that changed how this list is read.

  • The two facts that decide whether a ring is fairly priced — gram weight and hallmark — are published on none of the 13 listings. Both must be obtained in writing from the seller before payment.
  • The cheapest verified 14K solitaire setting here is $935 (Blue Nile Classic Comfort Fit); the cheapest 18K setting is $1,240 (Blue Nile Classic Six-Prong). They are different models, so the $305 difference mixes metal cost with design — it is not a gold-value calculation.
  • Setting-only and complete-ring prices are mixed together in most online 'engagement ring price' comparisons. Keeping them separate is the single most useful structural decision in this dataset.
  • Mejuri is the only retailer here whose pages use the words 'solid gold' explicitly; Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth state karat only. That is a wording gap, not evidence of plated goods, but it is the exact phrase the checklist asks for.
  • All three retailers publish a warranty (Blue Nile's is limited to items of $1,000 or more); none publishes a hallmark or gram-weight policy. Warranty coverage is not a purity guarantee.

The buy / no-buy rule

Buy only when the seller will put the karat, the weight in grams and the return window in writing, and will accept the ring back after an independent jeweler inspects it. If any of those three is refused, the price does not matter.

Nothing here is financial or legal advice, and no listing in this dataset has been physically inspected.

No invented numbers. Every price here was read from the retailer's own page on 2026-10-22 and is timestamped. Blank cells are blank because the seller does not publish the value — not because it was estimated.
