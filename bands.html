These are complete rings

Mejuri sells these as finished rings, so the price includes the gold, the stones and the labour. That makes them the only rows here whose price can be compared with a gold-value estimate — once a weight is known, which it still is not.

Mejuri is also the only retailer in this dataset whose pages use the words “14k solid gold” explicitly. That is the wording the checklist asks for; Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth state karat only.

Prices marked “From” depend on ring size. White gold pieces are rhodium-plated, which is a surface finish over the gold, not a lower-purity core.
