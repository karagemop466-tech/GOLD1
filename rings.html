How to read this table

Price (USD)
Exact listed price from the retailer feed. A range means the listed variants (usually ring sizes or metals) have different prices.
Price basis
Whether the price buys a complete ring, a setting only, or a starting configuration.
Weight
Metal weight in grams. Almost never published. Blank = unverified, not estimated.
Gold value
What the published weight is worth in raw gold at the spot price above: grams × karat ÷ 24 × gold price. It is a floor, not an appraisal — it excludes labour, stones and margin.
Last checked
The date the row was last opened and re-read at its own official page, and whether that re-read confirmed, corrected or could not reproduce the stored numbers.
Flags
Problems found while verifying. The full explanation of each is on Flagged for review.
Source
The official product page. Check it yourself before paying.

No hallucinated prices. Every price and specification on this site was copied from an official retailer feed retrieved on 2026-10-22, 2026-10-23 and is linked to the exact page it came from. Values that could not be confirmed are blank, not estimated.
