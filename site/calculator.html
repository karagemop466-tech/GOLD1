Formula and source

USD/g = bid/troy oz ÷ 31.1034768
fine-gold g = weight g × karat ÷ 24
reference USD = fine-gold g × USD/g

Full precision is retained until display rounding. The default is Kitco’s Live GoldPrice panel, Oct 22, 2026 – 19:11 NY Time: $4,025.00 bid/troy oz, displayed $129.41/g — a dated snapshot.
