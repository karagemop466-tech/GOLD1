Evidence protocol

Included

  • Direct official product and policy pages
  • Exact item/variant, price basis, and retrieval date
  • Field-level source excerpts
  • Independent BBB profile snapshots for reputation
  • FTC, GIA, CFPB, Kitco, and LBMA pages within their actual scope

Never substituted

  • Search snippets for direct evidence
  • Karat wording for explicit solid-gold wording
  • Listed item weight for net metal weight
  • Stamps or seller appraisals for independent testing
  • Benchmark metal prices for jewelry valuations
  • Missing values with estimates, zeroes, or conversions

Data dictionary

seller-stated
The official seller makes the claim. It is not independent authentication.
listedWeightGrams
The seller-displayed item weight only. It may include gemstones and may change with sizing.
netMetalWeightGrams
Verified metal-only weight. Blank throughout this audit.
Nominal gold math
Listed weight × karat/24 × dated 24K reference. Arithmetic only, not actual raw-gold content.
Purchase-ready
Requires every verification gate, including physical marks and independent post-delivery inspection. No candidate qualifies.
Active candidate
An official page was available at retrieval. Not a stock, future-price, or authenticity guarantee.
