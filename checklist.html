No hallucinations — the rule this whole project runs on

Every price, karat and weight below is copied from the seller's own official product page or feed and linked on the same row. Nothing is estimated, averaged, inferred or remembered. If a value was not confirmed on an official source it is shown as — unverified and listed on the Flagged for review page. Every build is mechanically verified by automated anti-hallucination and security passes (S01–S20 + A01–A10 + H01–H12 + N01–N11), and every row is re-read live against its official page with the result published on the evening live audit. We actively recommend that you look for hallucinations yourself: open any row's official product link and check our verbatim evidence quote against the live seller page before buying (latest re-read: 2026-10-23; snapshots are not live quotes).

1

Gold purity: confirm the karat — 10K, 14K, 18K, 22K, or 24K. Higher karat = more fine gold, softer metal.

verified

All 71 rows across 11 retailers state an explicit legal karat or 750/1000 hallmark fineness on the seller's official page, covering every legal tier: 4 in 10K (41.7% gold), 50 in 14K (58.3%), 11 in 18K (75.0%), 4 in 22K (91.7%, via Auvere), and 2 in 24K pure gold (99.9%+, via Menē). Under 16 CFR § 23.3(a)–(b), unqualified "Gold" means 24K only; alloys below 24K must disclose karat.

How it was checked
Karat read directly from each official product page/feed and verified against the row's verbatim evidence quote by automated checks S03, A02, and H01.
What to do
Choose 14K or 18K for prong-set engagement rings (22K and 24K are high-purity softer alloys best suited to bands or protective bezels; 10K is hardest with least gold), and verify stamp + assay on arrival.

2

Solid gold: the listing must explicitly say solid gold — not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay, gold-tone, or hollow.

open issue

21 of 71 listings explicitly state "solid gold" in the seller's own text (8 Automic Gold, 7 Mejuri, 4 Auvere 22K, Ross-Simons 997811, and Aurate Diamond X Ring); the remaining 50 state karat only. Critically, under FTC 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) & fn. 32, an item with a hollow center may NOT legally be called "Solid Gold" — yet Mejuri's FAQ and Bold Chain Ring ($348) call hollowed gold "solid gold" (flagged). Ross-Simons search results also mix solid gold with "18kt/22kt Gold Over Sterling" plated silver (excluded, Flag F57).

How it was checked
Checked all 71 product pages for "solid", hollow construction disclosures, and plating/vermeil/gold-filled/overlay terms (automated checks S05 & S07), cross-referenced against 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) and § 23.4.
What to do
Get one written sentence before paying: "Solid [10K/14K/18K/22K] gold throughout — non-hollow, non-electroformed, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled."

3

Hallmark: check for a 10K/14K/18K/22K/24K (or 417/585/750/916/999) purity stamp PLUS a registered maker's trademark.

not verifiable online

Zero online product pages display a macro photograph or verbatim transcript of the interior shank hallmark + maker's trademark (Cartier states "750/1000" fineness in text; Menē states 24K hallmarking on its COA). Under the National Gold and Silver Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 297(b)) and 16 CFR § 23.10, any quality mark MUST be closely accompanied by a U.S. registered/applied-for trademark or legal manufacturer name (with 3/1000 unsoldered and 7/1000 soldered gold assay tolerances under 15 U.S.C. § 295).

How it was checked
Inspected all 71 product pages and verified 16 CFR § 23.10 + 15 U.S.C. §§ 295, 297 verbatim on 2026-10-23.
What to do
Request an interior-band photo before purchase, and inspect under a 10× loupe on day one for BOTH the karat/fineness mark AND the maker's registered trademark.

8

Payment: use a credit card for statutory dispute protection; avoid wire, debit, crypto, or cash-equivalent BNPL.

verified

Verified against official CFPB Fair Credit Billing Act guidance (15 U.S.C. § 1666 & § 1666i; Ask-CFPB en-61 & en-1969): credit cards grant a 60-day written billing-error dispute right and statutory "claims and defenses" rights on >$50 purchases after a good-faith merchant attempt (statutorily tied to home state or ≤100 miles, though many issuers extend broader network chargeback protection). Debit cards, wires, Zelle, and non-credit BNPL do not carry FCBA rights.

How it was checked
Retrieved CFPB Ask-CFPB articles en-61 and en-1969.
What to do
Pay directly with a major credit card, save screenshots of checkout terms, and send written billing disputes within 60 days if misrepresented goods are refused for return.

10

Upon arrival: before wearing, engraving, removing security tags, or resizing, inspect at an independent jeweller inside the return window.

your action

Physical inspection cannot be done online — and return windows are as short as 7 days (Menē), 14 days (Anna Sheffield), 15 days with intact security tag (Auvere), or 30 days from shipment rather than delivery (Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth). 11

Professional purity testing: pair XRF with scale weight & specific-gravity density — never rely on stamp or surface XRF alone.

verified

GIA Gems & Gemology Lab Notes prove neither a hallmark NOR standalone surface XRF is sufficient on its own: in Spring 2024 (Vol. 60, No. 1), a 41 g resin-filled electroformed bracelet with a glued-on "14K" disk read 17.99K–22.47K on XRF with no plating alert; in Summer 2025 (Vol. 61, No. 2, "Gold-Plated Gold"), 9K/10K gold rings plated with higher-karat yellow gold read 13.5K–18K on XRF because X-rays penetrate only microns. Combining XRF with precision gram weight + hydrostatic specific-gravity (density) testing catches hollow/resin cores and under-karated interiors non-destructively.

How it was checked
Retrieved and verified GIA Spring 2024 and Summer 2025 Lab Notes directly from gia.edu.
What to do
Ask the independent appraiser for BOTH XRF fluorescence AND hydrostatic specific-gravity + calibrated gram weight, and keep the written test report.
