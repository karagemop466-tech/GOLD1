1

Gold purity: confirm the karat — 10K, 14K, 18K (or 24K). Higher karat = more fine gold, softer metal.

verified

All 59 rows state an explicit legal karat or 750/1000 hallmark fineness on the seller's official page: 3 in 10K (41.7% gold), 47 in 14K (58.3%), 7 in 18K (75.0%), and 2 in 24K pure gold (99.9%+). Under 16 CFR § 23.3(a)–(b), unqualified "Gold" means 24K only; alloys under 24K must disclose karat.

How it was checked
Karat read directly from each official product page/feed and verified against the row's verbatim evidence quote by automated check S03.
What to do
Choose 14K or 18K for prong-set engagement rings (24K is pure bullion softness suited to plain bands, 10K is hardest with least gold), and verify the stamp and assay on arrival.

2

Solid gold: the listing must explicitly say solid gold — not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay, gold-tone, or hollow.

open issue

15 of 59 listings explicitly state "solid gold" in the seller's own text (6 Mejuri, 7 Automic Gold, Ross-Simons 997811, and Aurate Diamond X Ring); the remaining 44 state karat only. Critically, under FTC 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) & fn. 32, an item with a hollow center may NOT legally be called "Solid Gold" — yet Mejuri's FAQ and Bold Chain Ring ($348) call hollowed gold "solid gold" (flagged). Aurate's Modern Cigar Band also defaults its base URL to a $118 Vermeil option alongside 14K gold.

How it was checked
Checked all 59 product pages for "solid", hollow construction disclosures, and plating/vermeil/gold-filled/overlay terms (automated checks S05 & S07), cross-referenced against 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1) and § 23.4.
What to do
Get one written sentence before paying: "Solid [10K/14K/18K] gold throughout — non-hollow, non-electroformed, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled."

3

Hallmark: check for a 10K/14K/18K (or 417/585/750) purity stamp PLUS a registered maker's trademark.

not verifiable online

Zero online product pages display a macro photograph or text transcript of the exact interior shank hallmark + maker's trademark (Cartier states "750/1000" fineness in text, Menē states 24K hallmarking on its COA). Under the National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 297) and 16 CFR § 23.10, any quality mark MUST be accompanied closely by the manufacturer's or seller's registered U.S. trademark or legal name (with 3/7 ppt solder assay tolerances under 15 U.S.C. § 295).

How it was checked
Inspected all 59 product pages and read 16 CFR § 23.10 + 15 U.S.C. §§ 295, 297 in full.
What to do
Request an interior-band photo before purchase, and inspect under 10× loupe on day one for BOTH the karat/fineness mark AND the maker's trademark.

8

Payment: use a credit card for statutory dispute protection; avoid wire, debit, crypto, or cash-equivalent BNPL.

verified

Verified against official CFPB Fair Credit Billing Act guidance (15 U.S.C. § 1666 & § 1666i; Ask-CFPB en-61 & en-1969): credit cards grant a 60-day written billing-error dispute right (undelivered, unaccepted, or misbilled goods, with payment withheld during investigation) and statutory "claims and defenses" rights on >$50 purchases after a good-faith merchant attempt (statutorily tied to home state or ≤100 miles, though many issuers extend broader chargeback rules — check your own cardholder agreement). Debit cards, wires, Zelle, and non-credit BNPL do not carry FCBA rights.

How it was checked
Retrieved CFPB Ask-CFPB articles en-61 and en-1969 on 2026-10-22.
What to do
Pay directly with a major credit card, save screenshots of checkout terms, and send written billing disputes within 60 days if misrepresented goods are refused for return.

11

Professional purity testing: pair XRF with scale weight & specific-gravity density — never rely on stamp or surface XRF alone.

verified

GIA Gems & Gemology Lab Notes prove neither a hallmark NOR standalone surface XRF is sufficient on its own: in Spring 2024 (Vol. 60, No. 1), a 41 g resin-filled electroformed bracelet with a glued-on "14K" disk read 17.99K–22.47K on XRF with no plating alert; in Summer 2025 (Vol. 61, No. 2, "Gold-Plated Gold"), 9K/10K gold rings plated with higher-karat yellow gold read 13.5K–18K on XRF because X-rays penetrate only microns. Combining XRF with precision gram weight + hydrostatic specific-gravity (density) testing catches hollow/resin cores and under-karated interiors non-destructively.

How it was checked
Retrieved and verified GIA Spring 2024 and Summer 2025 Lab Notes directly from gia.edu on 2026-10-22.
What to do
Ask the independent appraiser for BOTH XRF fluorescence AND hydrostatic specific-gravity + calibrated gram weight, and keep the written test report.
