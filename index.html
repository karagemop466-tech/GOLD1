Headline finding: karat is everywhere, the word “solid” is rare — and regulated

Every one of the 71 listings states a legal karat or 750/1000 fineness across 10K, 14K, 18K, 22K and 24K. 21 of 71 explicitly state “solid gold” in the seller's own text, while 50 name a karat only. Under federal 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1), hollow-center gold may NOT legally be called “Solid Gold” — yet Mejuri's FAQ and Bold Chain Ring call hollowed gold “solid gold” (flagged), and Auvere's 22K PDPs print size ranges as “Approximate Fine Weight” (kept blank rather than guessed, F54). What still needs you
