Before you pay

  1. Ask the seller to confirm in writing: solid 14K gold, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled.
  2. Ask for the metal weight in grams for your exact size, and a close-up photo of the stamp inside the band.
  3. Pay by credit card. Keep the listing, the chat and the receipt.
  4. On arrival, have an independent jeweller check weight and purity (XRF preferred) before wearing or resizing.
