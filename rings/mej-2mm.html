Before you pay

  1. Ask the seller to confirm in writing: solid 14K gold throughout (non-hollow, non-electroformed, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled).
  2. Ask for the metal weight in grams for your exact size, and a close-up photo of the karat + maker's trademark stamp inside the band.
  3. Pay by credit card (FCBA dispute protection). Keep screenshots of the listing, chat and receipt.
  4. On arrival, have an independent jeweller verify gram weight, specific gravity, and XRF purity before wearing or resizing.
