F01 high wording / specification

Only 4 of 43 listings use the words “solid gold”

Four Mejuri listings quote “14k solid gold” in their own product text. The other 39 listings — all of Ross-Simons, Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Anna Sheffield, With Clarity and the Aurate rings — state a karat without ever using the word solid. None of the 39 carries plating language either, so they are probably solid alloys, but “probably” is exactly what your checklist says not to accept. The Aurate cigar band even sells a vermeil variant on the same page, which shows why the distinction matters.

Affects: 39 of 43 rows (all except mej-deb, mej-db, mej-bde, mej-2mm)

What to do: Before paying, get the seller to confirm in writing: "solid 14K gold, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled, not gold over silver". Keep that message with the receipt.

F02 high missing data

Weight is published for only 5 of 43 listings

Metal weight in grams is the number you need to check price against gold value. As of pass P12, five listings publish one: the Aurate Modern Cigar Band ("Weight: 2.6-4g", a range) and four Ross-Simons rings that carry a "Gold Weight" line on their own product page (1.7 g, 3.8 g, 0.5 g, 0.5 g). That is up from 1 of 43 before the re-read. The other 38 rows are deliberately blank rather than estimated — the site never fills a weight from a similar ring.

Affects: 38 of 43 rows

What to do: Ask the seller for the gram weight of the exact size you are buying, in writing. Refuse to accept a carat weight as a substitute for metal weight. On Ross-Simons product pages the number is usually already there, in the spec panel under "Gold Weight".

F12 high physical verification

A hallmark and maker’s mark cannot be verified online

No retailer page retrieved — in this pass or the first one — states the exact hallmark or maker’s mark that will be struck inside the band. A photograph of the inside of the band is the only pre-purchase evidence, and it still does not prove purity. US marking rules are set out in 16 CFR Part 23, including § 23.3 on misrepresentation of gold content and § 23.10 on quality marks.

Affects: all rows

What to do: Ask for a close-up photo of the stamp. On arrival, have an independent jeweller confirm the stamp and test purity (XRF preferred).

F14 medium measurement

Carat weight is not the same as metal weight

Listings quote ct. t.w. (total carat weight of stones). That says nothing about how many grams of gold you are buying, which is what the gold-value comparison needs. 16 CFR 23.18 covers misrepresentation of weight and "total weight".

Affects: all diamond rows

What to do: Ask separately for the gold weight in grams. Keep stone weight and metal weight in separate columns, as this site does.

F35 low metal finish

Mejuri's solid white gold is rhodium-plated on the surface — yellow gold is not

The Mejuri materials panel says: "14k solid white gold pieces are finished with rhodium plating, giving them their signature color along with added shine and durability." That is a surface finish on a solid 14k white gold ring, not a gold substitute — but a buyer filtering for "no plating" should know the white version carries a plating layer that wears over time and is re-polished by jewellers.

Affects: Mejuri white-gold variants of the listed rows (the rows themselves are yellow gold)

What to do: If plating is a hard no, choose the yellow or rose variants; if you want white, ask the jeweller about re-rhodium cost and interval.
