Why these are published instead of fixed

A flag is a finding. Deleting an awkward row would make the dataset look cleaner and be less true. Where the seller's own page contradicts itself, both statements are recorded and the contradiction is flagged — the buyer, not the compiler, decides what to do about it.

F-01 High Needs review

Blue Nile 18K ring description contradicts its own metal specification

The Petite Nouveau Six-Prong Solitaire page is titled and specified as 18K Yellow Gold, but the product description on the same page says the ring is “crafted in polished 14k white gold”. One of the two statements is wrong. Do not buy without written confirmation of the metal from the seller.

F-04 High Needs review

No listing in this pass publishes the ring's weight in grams (0 of 13)

Gram weight is the single number needed to compare price against gold value. Blue Nile, Mejuri and Brilliant Earth pages publish width and thickness but not weight. Until a seller supplies weight in writing, the gold-value calculator cannot be applied to any row here. Ask the seller for the weight in grams before paying.

F-05 High Needs review

No listing publishes hallmark or maker's mark details (0 of 13)

None of the pages reviewed state what is stamped inside the band. Under the National Stamping Act a quality mark must be accompanied by the maker's trademark, so the absence of published hallmark information is a legitimate pre-purchase question, not proof of a problem. Verify the stamp in person and with XRF after delivery.

Affected rows
BN-14K-CCF-203284, BN-14K-TC-203288, BN-14K-LEAF-192442, BN-14K-BEZEL-296643, BN-14K-RIDGE-304497, BN-14K-6P-203272, BN-18K-6P-194159, BN-18K-PN-203268, MEJ-DEB, MEJ-DB, MEJ-BDE, MEJ-2MM, BE-18K-6P-121H6

F-06 High Needs review

Setting-only prices are not ring prices (9 of 13 rows)

Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth quote “Setting Price” / “Setting Only”, which excludes the centre diamond. A $1,000 setting plus a diamond is a materially different purchase. Mejuri's 4 rows are complete-ring prices, so the two groups must never be compared directly.
