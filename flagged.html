No hallucinations — the rule this whole project runs on

Every price, karat and weight below is copied from the seller's own official product page or feed and linked on the same row. Nothing is estimated, averaged, inferred or remembered. If a value was not confirmed on an official source it is shown as — unverified and listed on the Flagged for review page. Every build is mechanically verified by automated anti-hallucination and security passes (S01–S19 + A01–A10). Price snapshots are not live quotes: the latest product-page re-read was 2026-10-22; retailer prices and gold spot can change, so verify the linked official source immediately before buying.

F01 high wording / specification

"Solid" is not stated on 44 of 59 listings

Across the integrated 59-row dataset, 15 listings explicitly include the word "solid" in the seller's own text (6 Mejuri, 7 Automic Gold, Ross-Simons 997811, and Aurate X Ring), while 44 state a karat mark or 750/1000 fineness without the word "solid". None carries plating language except Aurate products that also sell a separate vermeil option (flagged). Under FTC 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1), "Solid Gold" legally requires non-hollow construction.

Affects: 39 of 43 rows (all except mej-deb, mej-db, mej-bde, mej-2mm)

What to do: Before paying, get the seller to confirm in writing: "solid 14K gold, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled, not gold over silver". Keep that message with the receipt.

F27 medium first-pass finding
