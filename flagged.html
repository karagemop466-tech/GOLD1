F01 high wording / specification

None of the 30 listings uses the word "solid"

Every listing states a karat (14kt, 18kt, 14K) but none of the retrieved product text says "solid gold". Karat wording plus the absence of plated/vermeil language is good evidence, but it is not the explicit statement your checklist requires.

Affects: all rows

What to do: Before paying, get the seller to confirm in writing: "solid 14K gold, not plated, not vermeil, not gold-filled, not gold over silver". Keep that message with the receipt.

F02 high missing data

Weight is published for only 1 of 30 listings

Metal weight in grams is the number you need to check price against gold value. Only the Aurate Modern Cigar Band publishes one ("Weight: 2.6-4g"). For the other 29 rows the weight column is deliberately blank rather than estimated.

Affects: 29 rows; only au-cigar publishes a weight

What to do: Ask the seller for the gram weight of the exact size you are buying, in writing. Refuse to accept a carat weight as a substitute for metal weight.

F11 high return cost

Return windows are short and returns are not free everywhere

Anna Sheffield allows two weeks from delivery and charges a 10% restocking fee (capped at $500) on refunds. With Clarity allows 30 days but charges $250 if the diamond certificate is missing and $300 to re-certify. With Clarity also refuses returns on jewellery 'worked on, resized or repaired by another jeweler', and Anna Sheffield makes engraved or bespoke orders final sale. A return is therefore not automatically free or unlimited.

Affects: with-clarity, anna-sheffield

What to do: Decide within the window, keep every certificate and the original box, do not resize before you commit, and photograph the ring the day it arrives.

F12 high physical verification

A hallmark and maker's mark cannot be verified online

No retailer page retrieved states the exact hallmark or maker's mark that will be struck inside the band. A photograph of the inside of the band is the only pre-purchase evidence, and it still does not prove purity. US marking rules are set out in 16 CFR Part 23, including § 23.3 on misrepresentation of gold content and § 23.10 on quality marks.

Affects: all rows

What to do: Ask for a close-up photo of the stamp. On arrival, have an independent jeweller confirm the stamp and test purity (XRF preferred).

F14 medium measurement

Carat weight is not the same as metal weight

Listings quote ct. t.w. (total carat weight of stones). That says nothing about how many grams of gold you are buying, which is what the gold-value comparison needs. 16 CFR 23.18 covers misrepresentation of weight and "total weight".

Affects: all diamond rows

What to do: Ask separately for the gold weight in grams. Keep stone weight and metal weight in separate columns, as this site does.
