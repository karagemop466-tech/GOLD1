The four rules that decide a row's verdict

  1. Solid must be said. 21 listings say it. The other 50 name a karat and never use the word, so a buyer cannot assume it is solid rather than hollow, plated or a vermeil build. FTC: "solid gold" means not hollow.
  2. Grams decide the value. 25 rows publish a weight, so the price-to-gold multiple can be computed for exactly those rows.
  3. A negative independent record blocks. The five Aurate rows sit on a BBB "F" file, so they cannot reach the shortlist however good the listing looks.
  4. Nobody publishes the hallmark. 0 of 71. That is why the final step is always a jeweller, not a web page.
